About

I'm a dual-degree student in Mechanical Engineering and Artificial Intelligence at IIT Bombay. I work as a research assistant at the institute and as a software engineer at Terrastack, building machine learning software that reads crop patterns from satellite imagery.

My interest is computer vision and its applications, from real-time detection on edge devices to remote sensing. I like projects where design and development meet an industry need, and I enjoy picking up new methods to solve them.

Outside work I watch anime, cycle and play chess.
